**29 Jul 2026 — cloud-eviction bug REPRODUCED end-to-end; fix list is small and exact.** Real project on Dropbox, three 307–649 MB screen recordings, Make online-only, Analyse → **run fails** with `ffprobe timed out after 30s probing <file>` (red ✗ on the row). ## Ideas (captured, not triaged)

- **Bare-key shortcuts don't guard modifiers — ⌘S/⌘H/⌘M also fire the bare-key action.** 5 Aug 2026: `frontend/src/hooks/useKeyboardShortcuts.ts` has one handler per bare key, and only `z` (`:614`, added with Focus Mode) checks `!e.metaKey && !e.ctrlKey && !e.altKey`. `s` (`:594` star), `h` (`:585` hide), `m` (`:499`), `x` (`:577`), `j`/`k` (`:570`), `[` (`:462`), `]` (`:477`), `/` (`:508`) match on the key alone — so in browser serve mode ⌘S stars the focused quote *and* opens Save Page, and any future ⌘-chord landing on one of those letters silently doubles up. Caught while wiring `z` (⌘Z would have toggled Focus Mode); fixed there only, deliberately — widening the guard to eight more handlers mid-feature was out of scope.
