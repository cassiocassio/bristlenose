**18 Aug 2026 — Settings ▸ Accounts SHIPPED, and the credential storage under it was rebuilt. Six review agents found four ways to lose a sign-in; all fixed. Read §7/§9/§10 of `docs/design-cloud-import.md` — they were trued in the same pass and two of them said the opposite of what the code now does.**

**The pane.** A section per service — Teams, Meet, Miro — with four states derived from disk and **no network call**: unavailable / not connected / connected / needs attention (two causes). That "no call" property is the design, and its consequence is the thing to carry forward: anything knowable only from a call must be *persisted when that call happens*. Google's account tier is free (it falls out of the address domain, so a consumer account is flagged before the researcher goes hunting an empty list); **Microsoft's `DriveTier` is not, and is the one state still owed a writer** — a personal Microsoft account still reads as merely connected. Pinned by a test named for the gap so it reads as owed rather than broken.

**The storage.** `KeychainHelper` keyed every item on a fixed account string, so a second sign-in took `SecItemUpdate` and **overwrote the first account's token in place** — no error, `set` returned `true`. §7 named this a year ago. Now one item per account, `kSecAttrAccount` a SHA-256 of the address, with enumeration and a one-shot migration that deletes the legacy item. **The address is the key, not `oid`/`sub`** — parsing the id claims was cut for v1; a rename produces a second row that is visible, labelled and removable. **iCloud sync STAYS** (§7 and §10 said non-synchronizable; both are now corrected): the stolen Mac is the realistic loss event, iCloud Keychain is E2E regardless of ADP, and `kSecAttrSynchronizable` cannot be flipped by `SecItemUpdate`, so the migration was the only cheap moment it could ever have changed.

**What the review found, and it was not the pane.** The finding logged **LOW** was the one that defeats the feature: `try? await client.refresh()` made a dropped connection indistinguishable from a refusal, and a tombstone strips the refresh token — so **a moment of bad wifi permanently destroyed a working sign-in and told the researcher their provider had revoked them**, on the aged-out-token path, which is the ordinary next-morning case. Four more of the same family, all one root (a key derived from a value that could regress): identity nil-ing on a transient `/me` failure and deleting the correctly-keyed item; the rekey deleting the *other* account when you signed in as a second one; Google learning its address on first listing and never publishing it; Teams having no backfill at all. Also: disconnect did not stop an in-flight transfer (`store = nil` cancels nothing), and Miro's disconnect left a working token in the sidecar's **baked env** and in the parked sidecar.

**The bigger finding is Teams, not Google, and it was in shipping code.** A `.docx` paragraph is **not a line** — Teams packs an entire turn (the `Martin Storey  0:04` header *and* every line of speech) into one paragraph separated by `<w:br/>`, which `python-docx` renders as `\n` inside `p.text`. A parser whose unit was the paragraph saw one unmatched blob per turn, so **real Teams transcripts also parsed to segments at `start_time=0.0` with no `speaker_label`**. Every constructed fixture put one line per paragraph — the shape the bug cannot occur in. The generalising lesson, now in `bristlenose/stages/CLAUDE.md`: **the unit of structure is the LINE, never the paragraph**, and any new fixture must carry the real intra-paragraph breaks.

**14 Aug 2026 — latent gender-agreement bug in every Romance `pipeline.json` (found during the Catalan review; NOT fixed, wants native calls).** `pipeline.json`'s `stageComplete` is `"{stage} <past participle>"`, and `{stage}` interpolates the 11 labels from `cli.json` → `stage.*`, whose grammatical gender varies. The participle is masculine singular in all five Romance locales, so it disagrees wherever the stage noun is feminine: **it 9/11** (`{stage} completato` — every `-zione` noun: Acquisizione, Trascrizione, Generazione…), **fr 8/11** (`terminé` — every `-tion` noun), **es 5/11** (`completado` — the `-ción` nouns; es/pt escape more only because half their stage labels are nominalised infinitives, which is its own register inconsistency), **pt-BR 4/11**, **pt-PT 4/11**. **30 broken renderings.** The morphology is mechanical, not a judgement call — `-zione`/`-tion`/`-ción`/`-ção` are feminine, `-mento`/`-ment` masculine — so the *defect* is certain even though the *fix wording* is not. **Not urgent, and that is exactly why it must be fixed now:** the `pipeline` namespace has **zero live consumers** — `LAZY_NAMESPACES` in [frontend/src/i18n/index.ts:31](frontend/src/i18n/index.ts:31) is `common`/`settings`/`enums` (+`desktop`), `I18n.swift:43` the same four, and no Python `t("pipeline.*")` call exists (the `t("pipeline.…")` hits in `SettingsModal.tsx` are a **key-prefix collision** — those keys live under `settings.json`, per `docs/design-i18n.md:244`, not in this file). `cli.json` is equally dead, so the interpolands are latent too. Nothing renders it today; wiring the namespace up would ship all 30 at once. **`ca` is already correct by construction** and is the model for the fix — `"S'ha completat {stage}"` puts the participle *before* a postposed subject, where it stays invariable; the generalisation is **never interpolate a noun into a slot that governs agreement**. Do not machine-translate a replacement: this needs the Italian reviewer (`project_boss_italian_formal_ia_reviewer`) and equivalents, and the Catalan locale must not be touched. **Same family, same file, also unfixed:** fr `stageStart` is `"Exécution de {stage}…"` → *"Exécution de Importation"*, missing mandatory elision on the 4 vowel-initial labels. **Adjacent terminology, flagged not fixed:** pt-BR/pt-PT leave `piiRemoval` as the English acronym *"Remoção de PII"* where it/es/fr/ca all localised it; and `done` leaves "Pipeline" untranslated in all five while `ca` localised it to *"el processament"* — a glossary call, not a bug. **No gate catches any of this** — `scripts/check-locales.py` validates placeholder *sets*, not grammar, and could not in principle.
