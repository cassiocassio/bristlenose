Which models through any-llm are good enough, cheap enough and fast enough?

Thirteen runs on the same three oral-history interviews, each scored against Claude Sonnet 4.6. Agreement is how much of the transcript a model's quotes cover in common with Sonnet's. Sonnet run twice agrees with itself 90% of the time, so no model can be told apart from Sonnet above that line.

Nothing reached Sonnet's own 90%.The best of the rest landed at 80–82%. Sonnet 5.5 is one of them, at the same price as 4.6.
Cheap and close means a known flaw.GPT-5.4 mini and DeepSeek V4 Pro reach 82% at about a fifth of Sonnet's price; DeepSeek Flash 77% at about a twelfth. Each one invents screens, over-splits themes or over-extracts.
The route changes the model.DeepSeek Flash native: 77% in 2m 20s. Through OpenRouter it thinks by default: 63% in 7m 19s. Qwen with thinking on never finished.
No model made quotes up.96–100% of every model's quotes are verbatim in the transcript. The failures are coverage and structure, not invention.
Read with care
  • One run per model (Sonnet 4.6 twice, for the noise floor). Gaps of a few points are indicative, not settled.
  • Three FOSSDA interviews (s1, s6, s10): public oral history, not usability testing. One IKEA usability session is shown separately below.
  • Agreement measures sameness to Sonnet 4.6 run A, not correctness.
  • Prices are list price × recorded tokens, not invoices. Cancelled calls record no tokens and aren't counted.
  • Runs span several commits on 23 Oct 2026; the Qwen "thinking off" row is after 8b16e797f.

How they fail

Invented structure

On interviews with no product screens, GPT-5.4 mini made 7, Llama 3.3 70B and DeepSeek Flash 6 each, DeepSeek V4 Pro 1. A quick tell of a model that fills a schema slot whether or not the data supports it.

Too many or too few themes

DeepSeek V4 Pro split the material into 23 themes, nine with 2 quotes; Sonnet made 12–15. GPT-5.4 mini made 3.

Thinking by default

Hybrid models think unless told not to, and the thinking is billed as output. A short probe on Qwen 3.8 Flash took 41.6 s and 1,839 output tokens; with thinking off, 2.1 s and 91. Its whole run went from killed after 38 minutes to 2m 56s. GPT-5 mini and Gemini 2.5 Pro refuse to switch it off.

A dropped field

Mistral Small left out a required field on one quote, and the whole session's quotes were discarded: it lost s1 in 3 of 3 attempts. Since 5939d6c89 the valid quotes are kept and the dropped ones are named.

The host, not the model

Free models failed at the host: a 404, a 429, and a Nemotron run that gave up after 29m 47s. A shared-pool rate limit ended Mistral Large through OpenRouter at 48 s. Llama 3.3 70B took 13 minutes on an 18-minute session.

Confidence without fit

Coverage hides calibration. In AutoCode with a usability codebook that doesn't fit oral history, Sonnet gave all 70 quotes 0.10–0.20 and excluded them. Mistral Large gave 0.3–0.7 to all 35, accepting 4.
