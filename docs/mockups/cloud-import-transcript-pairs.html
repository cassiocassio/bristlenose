A meeting is a recording and its transcript

Option A, chosen 1 Oct 2026. The transcript gets its own Transcript column, and one checkbox per recording fetches everything that belongs to it. A recording whose transcript is still being made waits: its row is greyed, its checkbox is disabled, Status shows a spinner, and it ticks itself when the transcript lands. The only way past the wait is the footer's Include {N} waiting on transcription. Drawn against the window as it is now: the scope is plain text with no capsule, Status is sized to its longest translation, and the window's minimum width comes from its columns.

1 Oct 2026 · proposal, revised after review and timing research · names and meetings invented · column widths are the shipped defaults (Meeting 320, Scheduled/Recorded/Expires 92, Size 74); Transcript and Status at their measured English minimums

Ask your IT admin to allow Teams transcripts?

Even for meetings you organised, Microsoft needs an administrator to approve before an app can read transcripts. Bristlenose asks to read:

  • Your meeting transcripts
  • Meeting details, to match transcripts to recordings
  • Recording start times, to line transcripts up with the video

If your organisation uses approval requests, Microsoft emails you the decision. Recordings keep importing meanwhile.

Commentary
  • Drawn as the window is today on a tenant without calendar access: no Scheduled column and no attendee line. The title comes from the recording's filename. The only addition is the Transcript column.
  • The sheet is 23% shorter, about 87 words cut to 67. It still names all three permissions. The email line is conditional, because not every tenant has approval requests switched on.
  • The bar is a system Label on a plain bar, not a painted panel. Its detail is one clause, because the sheet explains the rest. In the cells, “Needs approval” is glyphless secondary text, because the bar already says why.

  • Status keeps the video's reason on rows that are not yet imported, using statusKind (30 Sep).

  • The window is for recognition, not inspection (owner, 1 Oct 2026). A researcher picks the right call by day, title and the external names from the invite. The Transcript cell says only whether there is a transcript; speaker names and counts come after import, not here.
  • No empty columns. Scheduled appears because the approval brought calendar access and some calls matched an event. Calls with no event show a dash. With no calendar access (frame 1), no call has a time and the column isn't there. This is today's rule (showsScheduledColumn), unchanged. Transcript follows the same rule: if no row has a transcript to show or one on its way, the column isn't drawn.
  • Waiting is the default, and it is hard to get round (owner, 1 Oct 2026). A platform transcript with real speaker names beats Bristlenose's own guess in nearly every case, so a row whose transcript is on its way has a disabled checkbox, and Status says Waiting for transcript. Nobody has to read the Transcript column or a tooltip to see why the box is grey.
    • The row looks unfinished, and finishes itself while you watch (owner, 1 Oct 2026). Right after a session, nobody reads small text, and a bold title beside a checkbox gets ticked. So a waiting row has its title in secondary colour, a disabled box, and a spinner in Status: the same “give it a minute” cue the Teams chat gives. While the window is open it re-checks waiting rows (about every 30 s on Teams, where transcripts usually take minutes). When one lands, the row turns Available, ticks itself, and the Import count goes up. Nothing is remembered, and checking stops when the window closes.
    • One way round it, in plain sight, in the footer (owner's layout, 1 Oct 2026). Reading left to right: the count, then ☐ Include {N} waiting on transcription, then Project and the Import button, whose count is what the researcher will actually get. The checkbox is off every time the window opens and never remembered. The checkbox and its text are not drawn at all when nothing is waiting. Ticking it enables the waiting rows, still unticked; the researcher then ticks the ones they need, and the button's count follows.
    • Final states are never gated. Needs approval, Unavailable and No transcript mean nothing is coming. Those rows stay tickable, so an IT refusal never stops anyone working.
    • Bulk ticks never include a waiting row, even with the footer box on: ⌘A and a meeting's header checkbox leave them alone.
    • Recordings don't expire for weeks to months, so waiting costs nothing. When the window is next opened, the row reads Available and is ticked.
  • How “Expected” is known differs by platform, and the copy is only as sure as the signal:
    • Meet is certain. A transcript resource in STARTED or ENDED state means one is coming. No resource on an ended call means none was made.
    • Zoom is a good guess. The host's “Create audio transcript” setting is on and the recording is listed without its TRANSCRIPT file.
    • Teams is a guess. Recording starts transcription by default, but Graph lists a transcript only once it is ready. So Expected is shown only for a few hours after the meeting, and after that it becomes No transcript.
  • Expected becomes No transcript after 4 hours on Teams, and 24 hours on Meet and Zoom (measured from the recording appearing). Typical waits are minutes on Teams and a few hours on Meet and Zoom. Vendors allow up to a day. After that, the honest word is that it didn't come.
  • P05: the recording is here and the transcript isn't. Ticked, the Size cell shows the transcript's own size and says transcript, so the tick is honest about what it fetches. This needs the recording matched by a stored platform id, not by duration (plan §0 item 4, Q11), so it ships after the first slice.
  • The button names transcripts only when a tick fetches one alone. Otherwise it stays today's “Import N Recordings”, because a transcript rides with its recording.

Commentary
  • Transcript is sized the way Status now is. In the working tree (30 Sep), CloudImportStatusColumn.minimumWidth(i18n) measures every string the column can show, through a real cell. The grid then reports its minimum to the window (onMinimumWidth; floor max(760, grid)). Once that work lands, the measurer is generalised to take a key list, so Status and Transcript share one, rather than copied into a sibling. reportMinimumWidth already sums whatever columns exist, so it needs no change.
  • Transcript is added and removed, never hidden. A syncTranscriptColumn shaped like syncScheduledColumn adds it only when some row could have a transcript, so a Zoom account with transcription off, or a consumer Google account, pays no width for it. Removing rather than hiding stops autosaveTableColumns restoring a column stuck hidden.
  • Cost in width (estimated here; the code measures it): one more column of about 142 pt in English and about 175 pt in German (“Keine Sprechernamen”), plus an intercell gap. That puts the Teams window's floor near 820 pt in English and 880 pt in German, against today's 760. Meet has no Expires column, so its window is about 90 pt narrower.
  • Headers truncate; status words never do. “Aufgezeichnet” clips at its 72 pt minimum, as it does today. Names, titles and organisers stay unbounded and carry a tooltip.
  • The German state words are a first draft for review, not seeded strings.
