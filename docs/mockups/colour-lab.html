Commentary

  • Window geometry is read from the 2000 px screenshots, not from the app, so only the colours are pixel-true. The "Measured now" preset is those samples.
  • The shipped dark tokens are --bn-colour-bg: #111111 and --bn-colour-quote-bg: #1a1a1a (palette-default.css). The screenshot's dark paper reads #30312c and its side panes #1c1c1e, which are not tokens. Why is not yet checked; a native layer or the window material may sit under or over the web view.
  • Sentiment chips use the real light-dark() tokens, so they follow each frame's scheme, but they are not re-tuned when a surface moves.
  • Sidebar and toolbar are native SwiftUI in the app, and a material can tint them with the wallpaper. Here they are flat fills.
