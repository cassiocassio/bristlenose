What to try
  1. It opens on Keep Themes Sorted by Strength of Evidence (tick on). Click Add next session — new themes sort in; the divided “Pricing” theme lands low despite wide reach (strength of evidence discounts contention).
  2. Drag a theme — the tick clears (View menu + caption), you're manual. Add sessions: new themes append at the bottom, New badge; your order never moves.
  3. Re-tick in View to hand control back — it re-sorts and discards your arrangement (explicit, undoable-in-spirit).
  4. Delete a theme, add a session — it returns as New at the end. Simulate a merge in manual — a human-owned theme becomes a husk.
