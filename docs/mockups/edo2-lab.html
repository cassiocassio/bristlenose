Edo2 lab: shipped Edo against two candidates

Built from edo-website-palettes.html (same fragment, same computed contrast) and the follow-ups recorded in the header of palette-edo.css. Edo2 changes two things only: the card surface in light (yellow-cream to washi, the "warm surfaces" follow-up) and the dark ground (warm black to indigo). Accent, ink, semantics are untouched so nothing in the app moves under the researcher.

Constant neutral surround, as before. Contrast is computed in the page from the painted hex values; anything under 4.5:1 is flagged. The shipped dark muted (#7ba8a0, verdigris) is the one that looks greenish against the sumi; Edo2 moves it to a cool slate-blue to stay inside the indigo family.
