COMMENTARY · audit of non-error, non-warning project states (read off SubtitleVariant, PipelineSummary, the context menu, 16 Nov 2026)

The rule this proposes

Three kinds of "info", three homes. Activity is on the row while it happens and leaves by itself. News (the app noticed something you may not know, that changed since you last looked) earns the ⓘ on the row and leaves when you act on it. Standing facts (true for as long as the analysis stands; nothing to do) never mark the row and live in Get Info. The Zoom tracks are a standing fact. That is why they felt wrong as a row mark.

Open questions

  • Popover or window? Finder's Get Info is a window, Photos' ⌘I a floating panel. A popover off the row matches the two popovers we already have and dismisses lightly. Proposed: popover.
  • ⌘I looks free in MenuCommands.swift. Needs a check that the web report doesn't use it while focused. The menu-bar home would be the Project menu (or File, like Finder).
  • One "once" that is natural: the moment a run finishes is already a once. If the completion surface ever carries a line, "4 per-person audio tracks set aside" could ride it there, with no "seen" state. Not proposed now.
  • Order of work: Get Info is a new feature in a reliability phase. The smallest honest step is still yesterday's fix: tracks stop being failures (the study reads complete), and they appear as one row in the existing left-alone footer. Get Info could follow later.
