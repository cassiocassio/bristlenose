Commentary

This is what the owner's question draws when "the list is empty" and a sheet is kept. It tells the truth (the study is rebuilt from scratch) and claims no loss. The cost is the speed bump the Swift comment warns against: every Zoom or Teams study, every Re-analyse, one extra click for a researcher with nothing to lose. What it buys is a pause before an action that spends transcription and model time. That cost in minutes and money is not measured here.

Not drawn: a version that says the names "will be filled in again". That is a new sentence, so it would need approval and 21 locales.
