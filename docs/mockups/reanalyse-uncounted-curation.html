CommentaryToday a script's run --clean refuses here and says "Add --yes" over work nobody did. The database records how a person was minted (origin) but not who last typed the name, so I cannot tell a researcher's rename of a pipeline-filled participant from the pipeline's own name. I recommended counting only origin = pick, and that is wrong: participants are minted origin = participant, and the researcher renaming one is the commonest rename of all, so it would stop being counted.
