Research agent: the asker's experience

Companion to docs/design-research-agent.md. A quick sketch (Tier 1): hand-rolled styles, and every word is proposed copy nobody has reviewed. It draws what a PM, designer or engineer on the Product X team experiences, one frame per scenario; the ids (A1…) match the PRD's §7.

The chat surfaces are generic stand-ins for Teams, Slack, Figma Make and Claude, drawn only to show where the answer lands. They copy no one's interface. The quotes are invented.

Commentary

The tier difference from the asker's side (PRD §6). The agent pack answers well where the researcher pre-stated the number, and its suggested instructions tell it to admit the limit rather than guess. Ask a count the pack didn't state ("how many mentioned price and permissions?") and only the research agent can answer it. That's the step up the agent pack leads to.

Whether a company's agent builder actually follows "don't compute your own counts" is the first thing to test on a real tenant.
