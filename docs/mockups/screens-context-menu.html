Screens: the right-click, Mac app and browser

Tidy is the only thing the page adds: the board's way out of rearranging, on a custom board, wherever you click on it. On the Mac it sits at the top of the native menu, over WebKit's own items. In the browser the page draws it. Back and Reload stay.
