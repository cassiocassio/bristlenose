Signal snapshots: when one is written, what drift is recorded, and what the lens shows while it is frozen

The 100days [trust] row asks for three things: persist the last complete signal cards so the lens reads without Claude; refresh the affected cards when a new codebook is applied; and record internally that hand edits have drifted from the stored cards, while deferring the user-facing freshness UX. The first two are built. This page is about the third, and about the two moments the design doc leaves open: what the lens shows when it is frozen and no model is configured, and which later events earn a rebuild.

Artefact: what ships, real tokensCommentary: why, cost, risk

Built at HEAD (the doc still says “proposed”)

  • SignalSnapshot row, whole-payload JSON: source, payload_version, computed_at (migrations 023–024).
  • Three writers, all LLM-backed: codebook apply (routes/autocode.py), new interviews after run_completed, and a startup repair when the row is missing or on an older contract version (app.py). A build that cannot narrate every card is skipped and the old row kept.
  • Reader GET …/signals/snapshot never calculates; it filters hidden quotes on read (752cb6b50, 30 Oct) and only ever removes.
  • The lens is withheld while the first report builds; “Picking up Signals…” under the project name (75179d809, fde55d8f2).
  • active_project_groups honours a framework’s enabled state, so the doc’s §1 “split-brain” resolver is done.
  • Export embeds the filtered snapshot; the offline report reads the same cards.

Not built — this page’s remainder

  • No record of drift anywhere: no analysis_revision, no fingerprint, no ledger. The row’s “record that drift internally” is unmet.
  • A framework switched off stays in the frozen cards until the next LLM-backed run (the reader filters quotes, not codebooks).
  • No snapshot and no cloud model: the lens is lit and shows the sentiment-only fallback (bare cards), the state the owner walked and rejected on 30 Oct.
  • computed_at and source arrive at SignalsPage.tsx and are not drawn.
  • The coordinator, outbox, lease and candidate_partial (doc §4) — deliberately out of scope here; the row defers the repair UX.

Commentary Moment 5 is already right by construction: a frozen row is a complete report, and the reader does not need a provider to serve it. Moment 2 is the gap the row names: the inputs moved and nothing wrote that down, so a later freshness treatment, a scoped refresh, or even a log line has nothing to read. 2 · Decision 1 — what counts as drift, and where it is written

Every option writes in the same transaction as the mutation (the doc’s rule), at the five sites that already change signal inputs: a hide or unhide, a tag accepted, denied or hand-edited, a framework switched on or off, a section or theme edit, and a quote edit or removal. Opening the lens is never one. The nearest shipped sibling for “a mutation leaves a durable mark that a later pass reads” is AutoCode’s register of sent quotes (AutoCodeSentQuote): a row per fact, never a timestamp.

Commentary B is A with a name on each act. It costs one more column than A and gives the only datum a later freshness line could honestly print (“4 tags changed since”), and the only datum a build can log (“rebuilt after 7 edits: 2 hides, 4 tags, 1 rename”). C is the right shape for the deferred coordinator and the wrong time: a per-quote table is a second home for quote ids, with the export and deletion obligations that brings, in exchange for a scoping the row says not to build yet. D breaks the doc’s own rule and makes the read pay.

3 · Decision 2 — which events write a snapshot

A snapshot write is an LLM-backed pass: materialise_completed_signal_snapshot builds every card and narrates the changed ones (the elaboration cache returns the rest). The question is which later events earn one. The three that exist stay; the options differ on what is added.

Commentary A framework toggle is not a hand edit: it changes the analytical vocabulary, the one thing the follow-hide filter cannot subtract, and it is already an LLM-adjacent act (the apply that coded it). It is also nearly free, since every other card hits the narrative cache. That is why B is the one addition worth making now, and C is the one the row explicitly defers.

4 · Decision 3 — what the lens shows while frozen and no model is configured

Three states. The first two are the same cards with and without a datum line; the third is the empty case. The nav, heading and card anatomy are the shipped ones (signals.css, design A); the quote rows are drawn as bars so no participant speech appears here.

Recommendation. Record drift as a revision counter with a per-reason tally on the snapshot row (§2 B), written at the five mutation sites and reset by every write. Add the framework on/off hook as a fourth writer (§3 B), reusing the apply-completion path and the preparing flag. Draw nothing new on the lens (V0) except the readiness change for the empty, model-less study (V3). Leave V1/V2 as the deferred freshness UX, with the fields they would read already on the wire. Then true the design doc to “partial” and move its component plans A–E into a built/not-built table.

Open for the owner
  • Is a framework toggle a snapshot-writing event? It spends nothing when cached, but it is an LLM-backed pass started by a switch rather than an Apply. If no, drift records framework: 1 and the contradiction between Codebook and Signals stands until the next apply.
  • Does drift count hides? They are followed on read, so a tally of them says nothing the card does not already show. The recommendation counts them (cheap, complete) and would leave them out of any later sentence.
  • V3’s reason. Withholding with no words relies on the existing “No AI provider is set up yet” popover for the why. If the owner wants the lens row itself to say so, that is a new subtitle variant, not drawn here.
