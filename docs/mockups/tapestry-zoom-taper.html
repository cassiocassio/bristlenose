Commentary

This page is a diagram of the scale logic, not the product UI. Today is fitScale from frontend/src/utils/tapestryScale.ts, copied verbatim. Taper runs the same function after one extra step.

The taper. The band opens at 8 to 20 minutes, the values built on 9 Nov 2026. Each duration d is lifted towards the longest session L by a weight w that is 1 at or below the band's low end and 0 at or above its high end, falling in a straight line between: d′ = d + w·(L − d). fitScale runs on the d′ values, and the real durations are what get drawn. A lifted session can't be the "shortest" that sets the scale, and the change is continuous, so there is no minute at which behaviour flips.

If every session is short they are all lifted to L, so the scale fits the longest whole. That is the "short project shows everything" rule without a separate mechanism.

What to look at. The scenes show the opening view side by side. The sweeps below them slide one session's length and plot the opening scale. A step in a line is a cliff. Today's has one that is not from the taper at all: the "shorter than a quarter of the median" rule.

Bars are drawn at half the table's real width. The scale logic uses the real usable width, so the 4 s/px ceiling still bites where it would in the product.
