Decision trail

Everything decided, still open, parked or ruled out in this review, with the reason. The status words are new with this page: no earlier mockup had a trail format.

StatusDecisionWhy
DecidedWhere the SPA is the product, the toast system is worth doing wellOwner, 10 Nov. It owns concurrency, timing, pause and dismissal so no call site does.
DecidedKind glyphs in the web view are Lucide shapes in the palette coloursSF Symbols are for native chrome only: Apple’s terms scope them to apps drawing their own UI, and the project’s own rule (`design-icon-system.md`) says the same. Owner: “can live with that.” The shapes match the Mac’s symbols (circle-check, info, triangle-alert, circle-x).
DecidedA toast takes four of the five kinds; `skipped` folds into infoOwner, 10 Nov. The taxonomy keeps all five for the CLI and the Mac popover.
DecidedTitle and body anatomy, for toast and chipTitle in the emphasis weight, body regular and quieter; a one-sentence message has no title; titles carry no full stop. The cut is at an existing full stop, so it costs two locale keys, not new copy.
DecidedNo em dash in toast copyTwo short sentences, or a title and a body. Eleven values in the toast and chip set, one 21-locale pass.
DecidedThe wording, type and glyph slice goes into the existing toast firstIndependent of the engine choice. Order: this mockup and the doc, owner approval, locale and wording pass, title/body and Lucide, then phases 2 and 3.
LeaningOne toast look the researcher ever sees, whichever side raised itOwner’s argument against leaving the Mac toast (UX mixture) and against dialogs (annoying for small failures). Revised the same day: a native look-alike, not a bridge route, because some native events have no web view.
ParkedA native look-alike of the web toast for the four Mac sites (follow-up, after phases 2 and 3)Owner idea, 10 Nov. Same look, minimal behaviour, SF Symbols for the icon, a shared JSON contract to stop the two drifting. Wins over the bridge route because some native events have no web view to draw into. Full account in `design-notification-surfaces.md` §9.
OpenEngine: adopt Sonner, or harden our own and borrow its tricksDecided by a test-first spike: write the behaviour spec as tests, run it against both. Deciding facts: one speech path, size in the single-file export, works from outside React.
OpenHow the toast and the activity chip share the bottom-right cornerBoth are fixed there (toast 90 px up, chips 48 px up). A stack that grows upward would collide with the chips. Touches the chip-docking decision in the routing doc.
OpenHow long an error holds, and whether it waits to be dismissed“Not needy UX” and “failed things must be visible” pull in opposite directions (decision 4).
OpenMac app: suppress confirmations; Copy Screens successDecisions 1 and 6. Both need an owner yes before the page stops saying anything.
OpenInline validation for a taken name; “Open Settings” as a new stringNew UX and a new locale key, both behind the R114 gate.
ParkedA browser or Linux “while away” notificationDefault no before launch; revisit if browser and Linux researchers report missing a finished run (decision 9).
AbandonedSF Symbols inside the web viewApple’s terms and our own rule. A private-use code point might render on a Mac, but the same bundle runs in browsers and the export, where it would show empty boxes. Not tested, and not wanted.
AbandonedExtending `ToastView.swift` to match the web behavioursA second implementation of stacking, priority, pause and title/body kept in step by hand, for four call sites.
AbandonedMoving the drag-import failures to dialogsOwner: small failures, annoying dialogs.
AbandonedPromise toasts, swipe to dismiss, the expanding stack animationThe chip is the progress surface; a desktop tool needs no swipe; the motion brief wants causal, proportional, interruptible.
DecidedThe chip shows work as a shimmering label, not a ring or spinnerThat is the shipped chip (`atoms/activity-chip.css`: “replaces the old spinner”). The ring is native: `SidebarActivityRing` on a sidebar row. The first draft of this page drew an invented spinner; v2 now uses the real shimmer atom.
OpenTokenise the toast and chipToday only font size, colour, radius, the right margin and the toast’s z-index come from tokens. Padding, offsets, gaps, shadow, durations and the chip’s inverted colours are literals; the toast and chip disagree on z-index (1100 token against a literal 200).
AbandonedThe em dash as a separatorReplaced by a full stop or a title and body.
AbandonedA spinner ring on the chip (my first draft of v2)Invented; the shipped chip has none.
Abandonedv1’s placeholder “(same sentence, once it exists)” in the Copy as CSV failureNot product copy. v2 draws the key and says the wording still has to be written.
