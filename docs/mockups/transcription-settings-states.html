Commentary

Every frame below is drawn with Diagnostics on, the superset. Without it, Backend and Model are hidden and the engine card ends at the pop-up or the status row.

macOS 26+ also needs the speech helper in the bundle and the BristlenoseAppleSpeech flag on (the default). Either missing and a macOS 26 Mac gets the macOS 15–25 pane.

macOS 26 or later

Redrawn after the owner’s 12 Nov proposal: the first option is just “Whisper”, and the Whisper row speaks only when it has news, so the common case shows nothing under the pop-up.

macOS 15 to 25

No Apple model on these systems, so there is nothing to choose and the pane is as it shipped. Whisper’s download states are the same as above; two are drawn.

Decisions and open questions

  1. settledRow label: “Transcription engine” (was “Transcribe with”). A noun, as System Settings labels are; “Speech engine” was rejected because it also means text-to-speech. Landed in en and the 20 other locales on 12 Nov. The ru, uk and ko labels take the stem of the pane’s own tab name.
  2. settled“Apple’s speech model” stays. Formally SpeechTranscriber via SpeechAnalyzer (macOS 26). Its straight apostrophe, and the wider set of straight ones in en, is a typography sweep for another day.
  3. builtThe first option is “Whisper”, and the status speaks only when it has news. A condition in a menu item (“once downloaded”) read like a model. Silent when Whisper is on disk. Otherwise: Not on this Mac with Download Now, the progress with Stop Download, The download didn’t finish. with Retry, and Checking for Whisper… (new words, with an ellipsis, as every other in-progress line has). Under the Whisper choice the row does not repeat the word; under Apple’s choice it keeps it. There is no keyline between the pop-up and the status because they share one row: .listRowSeparator(.hidden) was probed and does nothing in a grouped Form on macOS, so the labelled Picker and the status sit in one VStack.
  4. settled“Whisper only” is gone (owner, 12 Nov). It meant no transcription at all if Whisper failed, when Apple’s model was a viable stand-in for most researchers. A stored whisperOnly from an older build reads as the default, so nothing renders blank, and nothing withholds the Apple helper any more. A test pins the stored value.
  5. builtThe detected line and Model are indented in the engine card and never dim. Apple’s option uses Whisper outside its nine languages and for any session its confidence gate defers, so the stored model still applies. The Model picker (and the macOS 15–25 Backend picker) is locked while a download runs, so the progress bar cannot describe a model you have just changed.
  6. builtBackend is a read-only line on macOS 26+: “Apple silicon detected”, with MLX as the consequence. Detected at run time (hw.optional.arm64), not hard-coded. The choice goes because the Mac sidecar carries MLX only (desktop/bristlenose-sidecar.spec). To keep the line true, overlayPreferences now exports only mlx as the backend (a stored faster-whisper is ignored; a test pins it), and the macOS 15–25 picker offers Auto and MLX only. The CLI keeps --whisper-backend and the environment variable as an escape hatch for a machine where MLX imports but cannot run.
  7. settledThe model row stays “Model” (owner, 12 Nov), not “Whisper model”. Known cost, raised in review: with Apple’s choice and Whisper on disk the indented rows hang under a pop-up that says “Apple’s speech model”, so “Model” can read as Apple’s. Accepted.
  8. settledBelow macOS 26 the pane is as shipped, less faster-whisper (decision 6). Design doc §8 records it; the §4 wireframe’s one-item dimmed pop-up is superseded. Whisper’s status stays in its own card there and is shown even when on disk.
  9. settledDiagnostics is reachable on every build: Settings ▸ Appearance, off by default.
  10. builtThe pane re-fits when the status row appears or goes and when the engine choice changes (the hint wraps to the selected item); checking has its own height value, apart from not-on-this-Mac.
  11. builtStatus probes no longer race. One runs at a time; a request that arrives during one makes it ask again with the preferences as they are by then, and every waiting caller gets the last answer.
  12. open“Read shared screens” sits on a Transcription tab although it is a screens feature. Its hint says “Never sent to the AI”; the design doc says the sentence also says it is not put in an export, which the shipped string lacks. The firm claim needs checking.
  13. owedNative review of the new locale strings in cs, fi, pl, tr and uk (seeded from each locale’s own vocabulary and Apple’s).
Commentary

Words in the frames are the shipped locale strings, except the dotted ones (“Whisper” as the first option, “Checking for Whisper”, “Transcription engine”, the curly “Apple’s”). “Download Now”, “Stop Download”, “Retry” and “Not on this Mac” are shipped keys; the progress line (“600 MB of 1.6 GB · About 2 minutes”) is the system’s own progress text, per the design doc.
