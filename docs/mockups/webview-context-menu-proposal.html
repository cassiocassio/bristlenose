The report's right-click menu: today and proposed

Each row is something you can right-click in the Mac app. Today every report link offers WebKit's full link menu, and its Open Link in New Window lands in the video player's window. The proposal keeps that item only where the page knows the place (project, lens and position), opening a real Bristlenose window there, and removes what does nothing or hands out a dead address.
