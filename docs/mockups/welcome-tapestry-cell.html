Session timeline — a Study tools slot

D is the chosen storyboard, built; A–C below are the candidates it was assembled from. Looping illustrations for a new slot in the Welcome window’s Study tools cell, teaching the timeline that opens under each Sessions row. Each frame is the cell at 800 pt of Welcome content (cell 490 pt, slot 458 pt). The illustration is drawn with the shipped tapestry stylesheet and the component’s own geometry. Words dashed in gold are new copy for the owner, not shipped keys.

Artefact — what ships Commentary — why

D · Unfurl, read, quote — the shipped template, at the Welcome window’s real slot sizes

Each frame below loads the exact HTML the app builds (WelcomeIllustrationHTML.timeline), extracted from the Swift source with its interpolations filled. Frames play in turn, like the baton in the app; the dark / Edo buttons at the top rebuild them.

What is shipped CSS and what is liberty. Colours, type, lanes, flags, marks, theme spans, ticks and the popover are session-tapestry.css, badge.css and the tokens, inlined verbatim through WelcomeTimelineCSS.swift (a generated file with a drift test). The scale rule is the lens’s own: fit the session to the width, held between 1 and 4 s/px, so a 40-minute interview draws as slivers and scrolls sideways in every frame narrower than about 700 logical px.

  • Liberties, each to fit. One theme row (the lens packs three). 34 pt of air above and below the sentiment axis (the lens uses 41 and 40). A one-row crop of the Sessions grid stands in for the real row. The scrollbar is drawn, because a programmatic scroll does not reliably show macOS’s overlay bar. The whole picture scales down, never up, when the slot is shorter than its natural height; the logical width grows by the same factor, so a short slot shows more of the session rather than a smaller copy of the same crop.
  • Read beat. One stop per section; the playhead sits, the tick nearest it turns blue, that section’s flag rises in full and holds for 0.8 s (absolute, not stretched by the group tempo). When the next stop is off-screen the timeline scrolls under the pointer.
  • Quote. A Delight mark in the last section, so the popover opens above the axis line and overlays the Themes lane, as in the lens.

A · The unfurl — the row opens and the session lays itself out

Session timeline

Open a session’s timeline to see who spoke, how it felt, and where each theme ran.

Read a session →

Teaches that it exists and where it is. A pointer clicks the chevron in the session’s ID cell, the row and slice take the shared tint, then the lanes fill in reading order: speaker clips sweep left to right in their scene colours, section flags drop, sentiment marks grow up or down from the axis, and theme spans draw.

  • The only candidate that shows the way in. The chevron sits in the ID cell, not in its own column, and it is small; this is the thing a first-time user would otherwise miss.
  • Slot height, measured: 224 (row 28, slice 174, scroll padding). The tallest cap in the pool today is ingest at 196. The rest frame is the open slice, so the cell does not jump.
  • Says nothing about what a click does. The timeline’s second job, jumping to the transcript, goes untaught.

B · Read and jump — the open timeline, under a pointer

Welcome ▸ Study tools · candidate B
Study tools
Session timeline

Follow a whole session at a glance — click any moment to open the transcript there.

Read a session →

Teaches the interaction. The pointer runs along the speaker lane: the playhead follows, the nearest timecode lights in accent blue, and each section’s flag raises in full as it passes. It then drops to a mark and clicks; the quote pops up under it, and the pointer rests on the popover, which takes its hover tint.

  • Every state shown is shipped CSS: .bn-tp-playhead, .bn-tp-tick.near, .bn-tp-flag.raised, .bn-tp-bar.sel, .bn-tp-popover:hover.
  • Slot height, measured: 232. The slice alone is 190; the popover, which overlaps the themes lane as in the app and is cut to two lines, hangs 39 pt below it.
  • Never shows the chevron, so the reader learns what the timeline does and not where it lives. The line has to carry “open a session”.

C · Open, then a quote — A’s first half, B’s last beat

Welcome ▸ Study tools · candidate C
Study tools
Session timeline

Open a session’s timeline to see who spoke and how it felt — click a mark to read the quote.

Read a session →

Both jobs, one loop. The chevron opens the slice as in A, but the lanes arrive together rather than one by one. The pointer then goes straight to a positive mark and clicks it, and the quote appears. No sweep.

  • Teaches the way in and the payoff, which is a quote: the thing every other slot in the pool is also about.
  • Slot height, measured: 252, the tallest of the three. The popover sits under a positive mark, so it opens just below the axis; under a negative mark it would run up to 26 pt further down.
  • Loop length about 7 s at tempo, close to Clips. The playhead sweep is dropped; hovering is the least discoverable part and the cheapest to leave to the app.
