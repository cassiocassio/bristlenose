The first-run Whisper download — what is left to bridge?

100days row "Whisper 1.5GB download — bridge with sample/tour" [Beta-must] [trust], folded from the 7 May walk. The row asks for something to do while the 1.6 GB speech model arrives before a first analysis. Since it was written, three things landed that change the question, so this page first says what the wait is now, who still meets it, and then lays out four ways to treat what is left.

The question, as it stands now

Given that the download starts at the disclosure and that macOS 26 Macs mostly never wait, who still meets the download with their first study, and what should that minute or ten look like? The old framing ("dead time, fill it") assumed a wait every researcher meets. Now it is a wait a minority meets, of a length set by their network, with honest progress already on three surfaces.

The 100days Demo dataset row (:1394) owns this; recommend moving the ask there and unpinning it from Whisper.

Recommendation

Pick A, close the row, and move the sample to the Demo dataset row.

  1. A now (S): the cover pane during the download carries Foundation's progress line and the shipped why; the stage verb becomes the pill's name, Downloading Whisper, in 21 locales. Three surfaces then tell one story, and a researcher on the slow lane can read how long from any of them. Nothing invented, nothing new in kind.
  2. Record the row as resolved by what shipped (D on 22 Oct, honest progress on 20 Oct, Apple first on 22 Oct) plus A. The "bridge" premise — dead time every researcher meets — is no longer true; the remaining audience is macOS 15 and the non-Apple languages, and for them the honest wait with a time is the Mac answer (Software Update does not entertain you).
  3. B is worth building, for a different reason: a sample report shows a new researcher what they are about to get, whether or not they wait. Hook it to Welcome (its own window, its own Help item), size the bundle first, and review it as a new kind (app-owned read-only project). Do not gate it on the download stage, which on most Macs never shows.
  4. C: skip. Welcome is already open on the launch that matters.
  5. Still owed from the Apple-first doc §8, unchanged by this pick: the by-hand check on a fresh Mac (r32), the gate measured on more real files, the pop-up labels, the two-fetchers case while Whisper only runs during the app's download.
