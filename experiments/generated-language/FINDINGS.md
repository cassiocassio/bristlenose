# Generated-language spike — findings

**Run 22 Sep 2026. 108 cells, 126 LLM calls, $1.04. No failures, no fallback output.**
Three corpora × two conditions × three providers × three passes. Per-cell tables are
regenerated by `analyse.py` into `out/compare.md`, which is **gitignored** — this file
is the durable record.

## 1. The steer works, on every provider, on every pass

Model-generated fields only; our own hardcoded label is excluded and counted separately.

| corpus | unsteered Spanish | steered Spanish | fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| escuela (native es-MX) | 0.0% | 100.0% | 195 / 198 |
| ikea-es | 14.0% | 94.1% | 186 / 188 |
| ikea-mixed | 0.6% | 100.0% | 172 / 168 |

One appended sentence on the system prompt was enough. Nothing the model wrote
escaped it. `ikea-es`'s 94.1% is the classifier's floor, not the model's: the
residue is short all-ASCII labels like `Aviso App IKEA` that the heuristic cannot
place, verified by reading them.

**The only English under the steer is ours.** `s11_thematic_grouping.py:130`
hardcodes `theme_label="Uncategorised"` and an English description for the
thin-theme bucket — 26 occurrences across the three corpora. It renders in every
locale today, steered or not, and belongs in `bristlenose/locales/` whatever is
decided about generation.

## 2. The unsteered baseline is undefined, and that is now measured

| corpus | Claude | ChatGPT | Gemini |
|---|---|---|---|
| escuela | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% |
| ikea-es | 0.0% | 25.7% | 16.0% |
| ikea-mixed | 0.0% | 1.4% | 0.0% |

Claude never produced Spanish unsteered; the other two did, on one corpus.
`docs/design-i18n.md`'s "whatever language the model picks, possibly inconsistent
between two runs" is confirmed, and the case for steering does not depend on which
language currently wins.

**A confound, found by reading the corpora rather than the results.** The three
corpora carry different *metadata* languages: escuela's quotes still hold English
`topic_label`s from its June run, while the Ikea ones were translated. The corpus
with Spanish hints is exactly the corpus where two providers drifted into Spanish.
So the surrounding metadata influences output language, and any future measurement
must control for it. It also means escuela's clean 0% English baseline is partly an
artefact of escuela feeding the model English hints.

## 3. The V1 cost is real, and asking does not prevent it

The steer explicitly said *"keep product names, brand names and on-screen UI labels
as the participants said them"*. It was ignored, on all three providers. The
`ikea-mixed` participants were on the English IKEA site saying "add to shopping
bag"; steered, every on-screen term was translated:

| participants saw | steered output |
|---|---|
| Shopping Bag | Bolsa de compra |
| Checkout | Pago y entrega · Proceso de compra |
| Product Detail | Ficha de producto |
| Search Results & Sorting | Resultados y Ordenación |

Not one survived. **If V1 wants on-screen terms preserved it needs a mechanism, not
a clause** — the natural candidate is passing the terms in as data rather than
asking the model to recognise them.

Secondary: Spanish labels came back in Title Case about half the time (`Bolsa de
Compra` beside `Bolsa de compra`). Spanish uses sentence case, so English
capitalisation convention rides along with the translation.

**Catalan disappeared entirely.** `ikea-mixed` holds a Catalan session, and neither
condition produced a single Catalan label — steered it becomes Spanish, unsteered
English. That is the single-language V1 decision behaving as designed, visible.

## 4. Stability: no cost demonstrated, and none ruled out

**A retracted interim claim, recorded because the retraction is the lesson.** At 49
of 108 cells the steered band sat below the unsteered band in five cells out of
five, and that was reported as a consistent direction. The full data reverses it:
on section clustering the steered runs are equal or **more** stable in four of six
cells. A five-cell sample from a run that was still going looked like a pattern and
was not one.

What holds at 108 cells:

- Section clustering is far steadier than thematic grouping (ARI 0.43–1.00 against
  0.07–1.00), in both conditions and every language. A property of the stages.
- Theme cells on Ikea are unusable and were predicted to be: 6 contextual quotes,
  2–4 groups, the catch-all holding most of them. Read Ikea for `s10` only.
- Escuela's theme cells put steered slightly below unsteered with heavy overlap.

No stability cost is demonstrated. The corpora are too small to demonstrate its
absence either, and a larger contextual corpus is the first thing to add if that
question turns out to matter.

## What this decides

The V1 design — one language, the UI language, by a call-site instruction — is
**supported**. The steer is reliable across providers, it does not need the shipped
prompt's `sha` to move, and no analysis-quality cost was found.

Three things it does not decide, all recorded rather than resolved:

1. **On-screen term retention needs a mechanism.** The clause does not work.
2. **Serve-time surfaces were not tested.** Signal elaborations, AutoCode
   rationales, codebook definitions and chat answers all generate at serve time on
   the *current* UI language, not the analysis-time one, so a project accumulates
   languages. That is a design question, not a prompt question.
3. **The per-session alternative is untested.** This spike measured compliance with
   a target language, not which target is right; `docs/design-i18n.md`'s leaning
   (the conversation's own language) would use the same mechanism and a different
   source.
