# Pain points: Smoke Test (3 sessions, 3 participants, 9 visible quotes)

**Caveat first:** this is a tiny study (9 visible quotes, 1 hidden by the researcher). Nothing here is more than "emerging". The overview shows `last_run: null` and no coverage note, so I can't say whether the analysis finished.

## How I decided what a pain point is, and what "top" means
- The project's codebook has a **"Pain points"** group, but it is hand-rolled (framework_id null), not the UX Research (uxr) framework, and uxr is not enabled. It holds one tag, **auth friction** (3 quotes: q-p2-200, q-p1-120, q-p1-300).
- That tag covers only part of the problem. So I also used the **frustration and confusion sentiments** (6 and 2 quotes), naming that as the fallback. I grouped by report section, because the report's signal cards are by section and sentiment.
- "Top" = (1) the researcher's star, (2) the signal strength from get_signals (a signal is several quotes about one thing, rewarding agreement across more participants), (3) quote and participant count. Bristlenose has no severity score, so which to fix first is your call.

## Top three

**1. Login: repeated sign-in, failed password, lockouts, missing code.** Section Login, sentiment frustration, group Pain points / tag auth friction. 5 frustration quotes, 2 participants (p1, p2). This is the only signal the tool computed (composite 0.76, confidence "emerging", flag "Problem").
- q-p2-200 (p2, s2, 03:20, starred by you): "Why do I need to log in again every single time?"
- Others: q-p1-120 "I typed my password three times and it still said wrong." (p1, s1, 02:00); q-p1-300 "The code text never arrived so I just gave up." (p1, s1, 05:00); q-p2-620 "Locked out again, second time today." (p2, s2); q-p1-0 "Login again? Ok." (p1, session s3, intensity 1).

**2. Login: expecting it to work like another app (confusion).** Section Login, sentiment confusion, tag borrowed model (group Mental models, not Pain points). 1 quote, 1 participant. Included as a pain point only because it is a confusion quote in the Login section, and it is thin evidence.
- q-p1-60 (p1, s3, 01:00): "I thought logging in would work like my other bank app."
- Note: q-p1-300 also carries borrowed model.

**3. Payments: can't tell whether a payment went through.** Section Payments, frustration plus confusion. 2 quotes, 2 participants (p1, p2). No tag and no computed signal.
- q-p2-500 (p2, s2, 08:20, confusion): "Where did the money go? It says pending and nothing else."
- q-p1-600 (p1, s1, 10:00, frustration): "I paid my sister and honestly could not tell if it had gone."
