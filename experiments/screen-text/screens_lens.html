Commentary

Generated by experiments/screen-text/screens_board.py from three real sessions. Every quote is placed on a screen by its timecode; a placed quote is pinned to one line of on-screen text when it shares a run of words with it, or, failing that, when a multilingual sentence model says they mean the same thing. Each participant's screengrab is the moment on that screen whose text pins the most of their quotes.

Stickies are the shipped quote card (blockquote.quote-card, the person badge, the sentiment badge), compacted. The blue wash on words is the read-aloud highlight: the words the participant said that are also on the screen. Dashed means another participant's quote carried onto this screengrab, because its element was matched across the two pages (same meaning across languages, same place on the page).

← → flips between participants on a shared screen; ↑ ↓ moves between screens. Hovering a sticky isolates it and its element.
