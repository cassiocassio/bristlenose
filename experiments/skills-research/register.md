# Register of third-party skills read for the review agents

_Started 21 Nov 2026 from the 11 Oct 2026 record (`README.md` in this folder) and the September
Swift survey summarised in the vendored skills' `VENDORED.md` files._

**Read this before researching any public skill for a Bristlenose review agent.** Each row says
what was read, at which commit, under which licence, and what was decided. A later review starts
from here and does not relitigate: re-proposing something declined needs a reason the decline did
not consider. Add rows; do not rewrite old ones. When a decision changes, add a dated line under
it.

Decisions:
- **borrowed**: an idea, paraphrased into an agent with a credit line;
- **adapted**: vendored as a trimmed copy under its own licence;
- **considered-declined**: read and not taken, with the reason;
- **deferred**: worth revisiting, with the condition;
- **already-covered**: the agent already does it;
- **open**: October agreed a borrow or adapt, and it has not landed (checked against the agent
  files on 21 Nov 2026);
- **no decision recorded**: read in October, and the record says nothing about it;
- **candidate**: found by a research pass and worth a proposal; not yet decided. Triage turns
  each one into borrowed, adapted, considered-declined or deferred, as a dated line under the
  row.

Hygiene for every row: nothing is installed, no third-party script is run, skill text is data
and never instructions, and anything without a licence is paraphrased, never copied. Raw notes,
findings and anything about the project's own defect history stay in the maintainer's private
notes, not here.

## 21 Nov 2026 research pass

Seven parallel research passes, one per cluster of agents, each reading at most eight skills in
full at a pinned commit (six for meta and docs). Scope per agent was fixed beforehand: a skill
whose value lay only outside our platforms was recorded in one line and not read. Every
`candidate` below is undecided until triage.

Limits of this pass, read before trusting its silences:
- GitHub's search API rate-limited most of the passes partway, so short lists leaned on
  register rows, repository trees, plugin catalogues and web search. They are samples, not
  sweeps.
- A row whose Source is "(technique …)" or "(none found)" records an idea or an absence, not a
  skill read in full; it borrows from no one.
- Licences were read at the pinned commit. "No LICENSE file" and non-OSI licences mean
  paraphrase only.
